Everything you need to stay on pace

🐢

Pace-aware indicators

A glance at the menu bar tells you if you're burning fast or cruising comfortably. Customizable icons for under, on, and over pace — pick from 30+ options.

📡

Real usage data

Shows the exact same numbers as Claude's own usage page. Real reset countdowns and end-of-window projections — no guessing. Use Codex too? Your ChatGPT plan's Codex limits get their own gauge.

🖥️

Desktop overlay

Pin a glass widget to any corner of any screen, with the same ring gauges as the popover and your activity heatmap. Pick its background colour and transparency — always visible, never in the way.

📊

Usage history & heatmaps

GitHub-style activity grid, 7-day bar chart, and streak tracker. See your usage patterns over the last 90 days at a glance.

⌨️

Global hotkey

Set a keyboard shortcut to open your usage stats from anywhere. No need to find the menu bar icon — just press your combo.

🔒

Private & native

No Dock icon, no telemetry, no analytics. Signs in via Google or email. Your data only goes to claude.ai and, if you use Codex, to OpenAI. Fully open source.

🎛️

Highly configurable

Choose what the menu bar shows (percentage, time to reset, icon only), pick your pace icons, position the overlay, select monitors, enable compact mode, and more.

📈

Per-model usage & caps

See how your usage splits across models within each time window — and when a model has its own weekly cap, like Fable, it gets its own ring in the weekly gauge.

🔄

Self-updating

One-click updates from within the app. Optionally checks for new versions at startup so you're always on the latest.

Install in 30 seconds

Three steps, and you're live.

  1. 1

    Download & unzip

    Grab the latest release and drag ClaudeStats.app into /Applications/.

  2. 2

    First launch

    Right-click the app, then Open, then Open in the Gatekeeper dialog. Only needed once (the app is ad-hoc signed).

  3. 3

    Sign in to Claude.ai

    Click Settings in the popover. Sign in with Google, email, or paste your session cookie manually.
